How the algorithm & road model work

Contraction Hierarchies: A background Web Worker collapses degree-2 street geometry, orders intersections by edge difference and hierarchy level, adds distance-preserving shortcuts, and returns a compact CSR upward graph. Queries run bidirectional upward Dijkstra with Stall-on-Demand, meet at the optimal apex node, and unpack shortcuts into real curved street segments.

Road & speed assumptions: Educational simulation using OpenStreetMap corridors and illustrative Vietnam/HCMC speeds (car motorway 75, trunk 45, primary 35, secondary 30, tertiary 25, residential 18 km/h; motorbike trunk 40, primary 32, secondary 28, tertiary 25, residential 22, alleys 14–15 km/h; capped by OSM maxspeed). Under Vietnamese traffic law, motorways are car-only. Live traffic, traffic lights, turn restrictions, tolls, and campus driveway access are not modeled.

Campus pins: Nhơn Đức, Đồng Nai, and Ninh Hòa use verified coordinates from supplied Google Maps pins; the remaining five HCMC pins are approximate entrance markers. Endpoints snap to the main strongly connected road component within 1.5 km.
