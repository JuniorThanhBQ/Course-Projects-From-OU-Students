How this simulation works

Fastest estimated route within the downloaded road graph. Times use illustrative road-class speeds, without live traffic, signals, turn restrictions, or conditional access. Motorways are excluded. Pins are approximate; the route begins and ends at nearby road nodes. This is an educational simulation.

Contraction hierarchies add distance-preserving shortcuts. Forward and reverse Dijkstra searches follow increasing node ranks, then unpack shortcuts into real streets.
