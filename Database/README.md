# Course: Database

## I. Description

This is a specialized course offered by Ho Chi Minh City Open University for students in IT (Information Technology), CS (Computer Science), and IM/MIS (Information Management/Management Information Systems). In my view, the course is relatively light in terms of practical work but challenging on the theoretical side—specifically regarding problems related to database normalization (1NF, 2NF, and 3NF). With enough diligence, you can pass the course with a decent grade.

## II. Tips

1. Don't skip the sessions with exercises (please don't). Pay attention to them because they're quite useful.
2. When practicing, broaden your questioning. For example: The problem asks to query exactly 100 people from a list who bought product A. Don't stop at the answer; ask, "If this list has 1 million or 100 million people, what's the most efficient way to get the first 100 people who bought product A?"
3. You don't need to delve too deeply into data structures and algorithms; focus on using "SQL" to solve the problem.
4. Use AI in the following way:
> - Do the problem first (utilize everything you have except the AI).
> - After you've finished, ask the AI ​​for its opinion to further expand your knowledge. It's recommended to ask it in the format: What - Why - How - When?
> - Absolutely do not blindly trust the AI's advice. You'll never know if its output is from a 20-year SQL engineer or some junk code found online.
